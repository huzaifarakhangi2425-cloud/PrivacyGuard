PRIVACY, PLAINLY.

Your answers stay yours.

The check asks only about habits. It never asks for your name, email, passwords, or account details.

Your answers and action plan are saved in this browser using local storage. They are not sent to a PrivacyGuard server. “Clear my data” removes them, as does clearing your browser’s site data. Your plan won’t automatically appear on another device or browser.

This site uses no third-party fonts, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. The hosting provider processes normal connection information to serve the website.

The dashboard reflects your answers and the actions you check off. It doesn’t scan your device, monitor accounts, change settings, or guarantee security.
