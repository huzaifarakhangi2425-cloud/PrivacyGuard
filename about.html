What does PrivacyGuard actually do?

PrivacyGuard helps you review five everyday privacy habits and build a personal checklist. It provides guidance and saves your progress in this browser. It doesn’t change your device settings, block trackers, or provide a VPN.

Do you scan my device or my accounts?

No. The check is a self-assessment based on the answers you choose. It doesn’t access your accounts, inspect your device, or measure your actual security.

Where do my answers go?

Your answers and checklist progress are stored locally in this browser, when browser storage is available. They are not submitted to a PrivacyGuard server. The hosting service still processes normal web requests to deliver the site. Use “Clear my data” in the footer to remove your saved plan.

Is there anything to download or pay for?

No. The privacy check and guides on this website are free to use in your browser. You don’t need an account, a payment card, or an app download.

PRIVACY, PLAINLY.

Your answers stay yours.

The privacy check asks only about habits. It never asks for your name, email, passwords, or account details.

This site uses no third-party fonts, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. The hosting provider processes normal connection information to serve the website.

The check is educational. It does not scan your device, verify your settings, or guarantee that your accounts are secure.
